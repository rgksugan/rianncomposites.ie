About Riann Composites

Built from hands-on craftsmanship

Riann Composites was founded by Antony Milton Raja, a hands-on composite technician with a background in aviation repair, sheet-metal work, fibreglass, carbon fibre, bonding and fabrication, together with co-founder Salvatore Aspanu Toledo.

Antony began his career in aviation working with sheet metal: bending, riveting and shaping aircraft panels. His interest in composites started when he repaired a panel using resin and fibreglass. The moment he shaped a strong part from raw materials by hand, he knew this was the craft he wanted to build his future around.

His first major composite project was a boat, designed abroad but brought to life through careful hands-on fabrication. That project showed him what composite materials can do when skill, patience and the right process are used together.

Today, Antony brings that same care and precision to campervans, boats, farm machinery, vehicle plastic panels and custom composite parts.

Who We Are

Built on Real Composite Repair Experience

Riann Composites is an Irish-owned specialist repair and fabrication workshop based in Co. Clare.

Our team brings extensive hands-on experience in composite repair, fibreglass, carbon fibre, adhesive bonding, plastic panel repair, sanding, finishing and small-part fabrication.

We focus on damaged parts that are expensive to replace, difficult to source or no longer available. Every job is assessed individually to determine whether repair is practical, durable and worthwhile.

We do not simply cover damage. We identify the material, prepare it correctly, reinforce it where required and finish the repair with care.

Our purpose is simple: to give customers an honest, professional repair option before they pay for replacement.

How It Works

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who repairs fibreglass near me?

Riann Composites is based in Ennis, Co. Clare and repairs fibreglass (GRP) parts for customers across Ireland. Workshop visits are by appointment. Send us photos on WhatsApp for an initial assessment before arranging a visit.

Can cracked fibreglass be repaired?

Yes, in most cases. Fibreglass and GRP cracks can be repaired using composite laminating, bonding and surface finishing. The repair method depends on the size, location and depth of the crack, and whether there is backside access. We assess every job individually and give honest advice before starting work.

Can a cracked boat hull be repaired?

Yes. We repair selected fibreglass and GRP boat hull damage including gelcoat cracks, chips, small holes and impact damage. Large or structural hull repairs require a workshop inspection before we can accept the job. Send us photos for an honest first opinion.

Who repairs fibreglass boats in Ireland?

Riann Composites repairs fibreglass and GRP boats in Co. Clare. We repair gelcoat damage, cracks, small hull damage, hatch covers and deck panels. Contact us with photos for a free repair opinion.

How much does fibreglass boat repair cost?

Cost depends on the size of the damage, the repair method required and access to the damaged area. We give a clear quote before any work begins. Send us photos on WhatsApp for a free initial repair opinion.

Can a cracked carbon bicycle frame be repaired?

Selected carbon bicycle repairs are possible, including cosmetic cracking and surface damage. Safety-critical structural frame damage may be refused where repair is not appropriate. Send us photos for an honest assessment before arranging a workshop visit.

Who repairs carbon bike frames in Ireland?

Riann Composites repairs selected carbon fibre bicycle frames and components in Co. Clare, Ireland. We assess each frame individually. Structural cracks that compromise safety may be declined if repair is not the right option.

Who repairs campervan fibreglass in Ireland?

We repair selected GRP and fibreglass panels on campervans, motorhomes and caravans in Co. Clare, including cracks, holes, corner damage, roof edge damage and access panels. Workshop visits by appointment.

Can a damaged motorhome panel be repaired?

Yes, in many cases. GRP and fibreglass panels on motorhomes and caravans can be repaired rather than replaced. We inspect the damage and give honest advice on whether repair or replacement is the better option.

Do you repair tractor plastic panels?

Yes, depending on the plastic type and damage. We repair selected tractor, machinery and vehicle plastic panels using plastic welding, hot staples, reinforcement mesh and flexible finishing methods where suitable.

Can every damaged part be repaired?

No. Some parts are better replaced. We inspect the damage and give honest advice before work starts. If replacement is the safer or more practical option, we will tell you.

Do I need to bring the part to you?

Most repairs are workshop-based and by appointment only in Co. Clare. Mobile inspection or collection may be discussed depending on the job and location.

Do you paint after repair?

Finishing and paint preparation can be discussed. Full paint matching may be arranged case by case depending on the job.
